How it plays

18 things to grow

From turnips and carrots to dairy cows, truffle pigs and comet fruit, then a farm shop, a cannery and an army of robot harvesters.

Farm Hands never clock out

Hire one for every crop. They keep harvesting while the game is closed, for up to 12 hours, and longer with upgrades.

Ship the harvest

Sell the season’s crop and start over, stronger. Golden Seeds make every future harvest worth more, and each one is a Research Point for the skill tree.

A song for every season

Spring, summer, autumn and winter each have their own theme, and the farm turns into a starry night after dark. The music is all synthesized in code.

Dress up your farm

Hats, outfits, pets, garden gnomes and a hot-air balloon. Earn them by playing or pick your favourites with gems: they only change how the farm looks.

Events and a weekly race

Limited-time events like Market Day, and an optional weekly leaderboard with generated farm names. Gems save time but never decide a race.

Daily and weekly missions · 50+ achievements · A 7-day gift streak · The Golden Crow

Take a look

Free, and fair about it

Farm Capitalist is free. The ads are optional rewarded videos that you only watch when you want a boost. Optional purchases are gem packs, a one-time Starter Pack and the VIP Farm Club, a monthly subscription you can cancel in Google Play at any time. You do not need an account, and your farm is saved on your phone. Cloud save is optional.
